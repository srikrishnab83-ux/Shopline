Return & Exchange Policy

Delivery all over Kerala

✅ You can cancel order within 24 hours if ordered wrongly - No questions asked!

1. Cancellation

  • Cancel within 24 hours from order placement if ordered by mistake, wrong size/color, etc.
  • Go to My Orders → Click Cancel Order → Select reason
  • Refund (if prepaid) processed within 2-3 days
  • After 24 hours or if shipped, cancellation not possible. Contact support via Contact Us chatbot.

2. Return Policy - 3 Days

  • 3 days return available for eligible products (Fashion, damaged, wrong item)
  • Product should be unused, with tags, original packaging
  • Some products non-returnable: Innerwear, perishable, personal care, custom items

3. How to Return

  • Go to My Orders → Select Return (if eligible) or contact via Contact Us AI chatbot
  • Share reason and images if damaged
  • Our team will arrange pickup - Delivery all over Kerala
  • Refund after quality check - 3-5 working days

4. Exchange

Exchange available for size issues in Fashion within 3 days. Contact support via Contact Us page.

5. Refund

Refund to original payment method or wallet. COD orders refunded via UPI/Bank transfer.

6. Contact for Return

Use Contact Us - AI Chatbot or email support@shoplinekerala.com with Order ID
